Added TECSUN-style ETM+ mode on AM shortwave bands alongside regular ETM. ETM+ Scan stores the current band's stations in one of 24 local-hour lists (E00 through E23), preserves the previous list when cancelled, and selects ETM+ when complete. Tuning uses the displayed local hour's persistent list, which is identified by its E-hour above the frequency unit. While ETM or ETM+ is active, selecting an AM shortwave band switches to ETM+ when the clock is set and to regular ETM otherwise; selecting an unsupported band also uses regular ETM.
