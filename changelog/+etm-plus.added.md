Added TECSUN-style ETM+ behavior to the unified ETM mode on AM shortwave bands. ETM Scan stores the current band's stations in one of 24 local-hour lists (E00 through E23), preserves the previous list when cancelled, and selects ETM when complete. Tuning automatically uses the displayed local hour's persistent list, which is identified by its E-hour above the frequency unit. Changing bands while ETM is active switches between ETM+ on AM shortwave and regular ETM on other bands.
